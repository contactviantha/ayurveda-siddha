Traditional Siddha & Ayurveda

At Vaidyanatha Clinic, every treatment begins with a thorough Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis) — the cornerstone of Ayurvedic assessment. Our physicians spend 20–30 minutes understanding your constitution (Prakriti) before prescribing any remedy.

We source our herbal medicines from certified organic farms in Kerala and the Nilgiris, ensuring every preparation retains its full therapeutic potency.

Our Signature Treatments

Authentic Ayurvedic and Siddha therapies, administered by certified physicians using traditionally prepared medicines.

Panchakarma Detox

A five-step cleansing protocol that eliminates deep-seated toxins (Ama) and restores Dosha balance. 7–21 day intensive programmes available.

Signature Programme

Kizhi Therapy

Warm herbal pouches (Podikizhi, Ilakizhi, Njavara Kizhi) gently massaged over the body to relieve joint pain, stiffness and muscular tension.

Joint & Pain Relief

Shirodhara

A continuous stream of warm medicated oil poured on the forehead — deeply relaxing for insomnia, anxiety, migraines and neurological conditions.

Stress & Mind

Njavara / Shastica Shali

Special rice cooked with milk and herbs, applied warm on the body. Highly effective for neuromuscular disorders, paralysis recovery and skin conditions.

Rejuvenation

Siddha Marma Therapy

Activation of vital energy points (Marma) unique to Tamil Siddha tradition — balances Pranic flow, aids in chronic pain, fatigue and immune disorders.

Siddha Exclusive

Women's Wellness

Specialised treatments for PCOS, menstrual disorders, prenatal/postnatal care, and menopausal health using classical Ayurvedic formulations.
